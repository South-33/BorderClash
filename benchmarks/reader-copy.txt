Cambodia protested these actions, supports ASEAN ceasefire monitoring, and is joining talks with Thailand in Beijing to reduce tension.

4. Displacement: Cambodia says nearly 8,000 people remain displaced along the Thai border despite the ceasefire.

5. Diplomacy: China hosted informal talks intended to help Cambodia and Thailand rebuild trust.
